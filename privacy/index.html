Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Quiet Museum: Jigsaw Puzzles has not been released yet. This policy describes how the app will handle information when it launches. We will update this page before launch if anything changes.

This policy explains what information is collected when you use the Quiet Museum: Jigsaw Puzzles app for Android (the "app") and the website quietmuseum.com (the "website"). Both are made by Quiet Museum, an independent game developer ("we", "us").

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts.
  • Your puzzle progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your puzzle progress, completed paintings, settings (such as display size and sound), and a record of the purchases you have made. This information stays on your device. We do not send it to our own servers, and we do not have access to it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not bought "Remove ads" or "Patron", the app shows occasional ads between puzzles, and offers optional ads you can choose to watch for rewards. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • information about your device, such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's privacy policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads are limited to a family-friendly rating, and the app never shows ads while a puzzle is on screen.

Your advertising choices

  • If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the app will ask for your consent before ads use your information. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact wording varies by phone).
  • Buying "Remove ads" or "Patron" stops the ads between puzzles.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your card or bank details. Google Play tells the app which products you own so that your purchases can be unlocked and restored.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and keep it only as long as needed to help you.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The app is designed for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and purchase services described above, we do not share information with anyone.

Security

Information sent between the app and Google's ad and purchase services is encrypted in transit.

Deleting your information

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your phone's settings. To ask us to delete an email conversation, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
