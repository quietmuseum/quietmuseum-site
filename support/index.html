Support

Need help, found a problem, or have an idea? Email us at quietmuseumgames@gmail.com. A real person reads every message, and we aim to reply within three business days.

It helps if you tell us your phone or tablet model and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot is welcome too.

Common questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Every puzzle saves automatically as you go. Leave whenever you like, and tap Continue on the home screen to pick up exactly where you stopped. Each painting keeps its own saved puzzle.

Will I lose my progress if I uninstall the app?

Yes. Progress is stored only on your device, so uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. Your purchases are not lost; see the next question.

How do I get my purchases back on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you used to buy them, install the app, then open Settings → Restore purchases. Purchases are also restored automatically when the app starts.

Can I make the pieces or text bigger?

Yes. Choose fewer pieces (12 or 24 give the largest pieces), and use Settings → Display size to make everything larger. You can also zoom in with the + button while you play, and turn on High contrast if that's easier on your eyes.

Pieces are hard to fit. Can the game help?

Go to Settings → Snap help and choose Generous or Extra. Pieces will then click into place from farther away. During a puzzle, Edges shows only the edge pieces, Preview shows the finished picture, and Hint places a piece for you.

When do ads appear?

Never during a puzzle. An occasional short ad may appear after you finish a puzzle, and some optional ads let you earn rewards if you choose to watch them. A one-time purchase removes the ads between puzzles.

Is there a subscription?

No. Every purchase is a one-time payment.

I was charged by mistake. Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Google Play, so refunds are handled by Google. Follow Google's guide to requesting a refund on Google Play. You're welcome to email us too and we'll help where we can.
